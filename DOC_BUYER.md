## 1. Shopping Experience
Browse through curated jute collections on the homepage. Click on any product to see high-quality images and detailed variants.

## 2. Search & Filtering
- **Global Search**: Use the search bar in the Navbar to find specific items.
- **Categories**: Filter products by their natural material types.
- **Sorting**: Sort products by price (Low to High/High to Low) or see the **Newest First**.

## 3. Your Account
Login or sign up to:
- Track your order history.
- Save your profile information.
- Manage your shopping cart across sessions.

## 4. Ordering Process
Add items to your cart, review your choices, and proceed to checkout to place your order with eco-friendly sellers.
