## 1. Overview
As an **Owner**, you have full control over the storefront. Your primary dashboard is the **Owner Dashboard**, where you can track sales, products, and customer queries.

## 2. Product Management
You can add, edit, or delete anything in your catalog:
- **Details**: Edit names, descriptions, and categories.
- **Pricing**: Manage original and discounted prices.
- **Inventory**: Update stock counts and size/color variants.
- **Highlights**: Use the **"Show in Banner"** toggle to feature a product in the homepage slider.
- **Images**: Upload multiple PNG or JPG images.

## 3. Order Processing
View and manage customer orders:
- Track status (Pending, Shipped, Delivered).
- View customer details and delivery estimates.

## 4. Banners & Branding
Customize the homepage experience by managing promotional banners.

## 5. Inquiries & Customer Support
Respond to customer questions and messages directly from the panel.

## 6. Store Settings
Manage your social media links (Instagram, etc.) and store contact information.
